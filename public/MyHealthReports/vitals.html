Body measurements

Weight, waist, blood pressure, pulse, sleep, steps and home sugar readings. These are what change first when insulin resistance and cholesterol improve, long before the next blood test. One row per day; fill only what you measured.

Import from a CSV

Exports from a smart scale, BP monitor or fitness app work if they have a Date column; columns named like Weight, Waist, Systolic, Diastolic, Pulse / Heart rate, Sleep, Steps or Glucose are picked up. Days already entered are merged, not duplicated. Dates like 25/10/2026 are read as day/month.
